# Developer-Focused Resource

## Overview

Developer relations is judged on whether developers can follow you.
This Goal is one resource, published to the standard you would put your name to,
about a technology in the Holdex ecosystem.

## Objective

Help a developer understand and start using a specific tool, API, SDK,
or framework that Holdex or TRUF.Network works with.

## Choosing and building the resource

Pick the technology first, then produce either:

1. A written post or tutorial carrying a clear problem statement or use case,
   step by step instructions with code, and the practices worth knowing.
1. A short video, five to ten minutes, covering the same ground.

A repository with a working example alongside it is welcome.

## Engaging the reader

Close with something for the reader to do,
and suggest how they would extend the example
or solve the neighbouring problem themselves.

Publishing it, on Dev.to, Medium, or X, and answering the replies, is a bonus.
Mention Holdex if you do.

## What is assessed

1. Whether the technical detail is correct and current.
1. Whether a developer of any level can follow the explanation.
1. Whether it reads as written for developers rather than at them.
1. Whether it would inspire someone to try the technology.
1. Whether it is finished enough to put in front of a real audience.
