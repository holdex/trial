# Developers Coaching Dashboard

## Overview

Holdex treats engineers the way a team treats athletes,
and coaching is part of that.
Coaching needs evidence,
so we track how work actually goes and keep the history.
This Goal turns that history into something a person can act on.

## Objective

Turn Holdex performance history into forecasts, pictures,
and a framed business question that a stakeholder can decide from.

## Getting the data

You work with real Holdex historical data.
It is handed to you once you have created your repository
and pinged the reviewers on your Goal issue,
as described in [how the trial runs](../trial.md).

## Forecasting performance

Build a model that forecasts developer performance from the history.

Report how you chose the model, what you engineered from the raw data,
how it performs, and what a business stakeholder should take from it.

## Showing what the data says

Analyse the history and show the trends that matter,
as a presentation or an interactive dashboard,
ending in recommendations for improving engagement.

## Framing a business question

Take a business scenario, improving developer productivity for example,
and frame it as a data science question.

State the problem, the metrics that would count as success,
the data you would need, and how you would go about the analysis and modelling.
