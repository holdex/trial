# Executive Assistant Assessment

## Overview

This role is judged on range rather than on one piece of work,
so the trial starts with an honest self-assessment.
A small assignment follows, chosen from what your answers show.

## Objective

Establish where you are strong and where you are still learning,
in your own words, before anyone spends time on an assignment.

## Rating yourself

Give a separate rating from 0 to 10 for every skill listed below. Do not combine
several skills into one rating. Add a note wherever a number alone would
mislead:

| Rating | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | No experience |
| 1 to 3 | Basic familiarity |
| 4 to 6 | Can do it with some supervision |
| 7 to 9 | Can do it independently and well |
| 10 | Can train others |

**Administrative and organisational**:
managing complex calendars and resolving conflicts;
triaging an inbox and drafting correspondence;
juggling deadlines and knowing what is urgent;
organising and retrieving documents;
handling private information with discretion.

**Technical**: Microsoft Office; Google Workspace;
communication tools such as Telegram, Teams, or Zoom; task tools such as GitHub,
Asana, Trello, or Monday; expense and travel systems.

**Events and logistics**: domestic travel; international travel,
including visas and complex itineraries;
internal meetings and small external events.

**Problem solving**: troubleshooting without supervision;
adapting to shifting priorities; researching and summarising.
