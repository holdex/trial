# Holdex Services

## Overview

Holdex does twelve different things,
and a visitor to the site cannot easily tell what they are or
which one they need.
This Goal is the design that fixes that.

## Objective

Let a visitor to holdex.io understand what Holdex does
and recognise the service they came for.

## The services

1. Product co-building
1. Product design
1. EVM and SVM smart contracts
1. Extensions and plugins
1. Security audit
1. Tokenization of RWA
1. Node infrastructure development
1. Node operator
1. AI integration
1. Hiring and training
1. Marketing and growth
1. Consulting and advisory

## Deliverables

1. A short strategy document on how you would collaborate on this Goal while
   holding to the [Developer Guidelines](https://github.com/holdex/developers).
1. A design in Figma, low or high fidelity, that achieves the objective.
1. A walkthrough of your decisions, written or on video, five minutes at most.
